Top 5 Video Games

#3 Minecraft

  • Minecraft was created in 2009 and released to computers in 2011. It is a sandbox game where players can build and explore virtual worlds. It has various games modes like survival, creative, and adventure. It also has online servers for example Hypixel.

    • Games Modes

    1. Survival mode is where players gather resources like wood, stone, coal, etc to survive and progress throughout the game.

    2. Creative mode is where players have unlimited resources and can freely build and create without any limitations.

    3. Adventure mode is where players can explore custom maps and complete challenges set by other players.

    Online Servers

    Online servers allow players to connect and play with others around the world. There are a numerous amount of online servers but these are the most popular across all platforms.

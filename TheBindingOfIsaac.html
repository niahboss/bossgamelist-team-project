The Binding Of Isaac

The 2nd best game would be The Binding of Isaac. It is a roguelike action-adventure game developed by Edmund McMillen and Florian Hammerschmidt. It follows Isaac, a young boy who must navigate through procedurally generated dungeons to escape.

Creation

The Project was initially conceived as a small indie game taking 3 months to develop on Adobe Flash. In fact this game was supposed to be a prototype for a larger project, but it ended up becoming a standalone game in its own right. Edmund Wasn't Expecting the game to gain such popularity and recognition but players were loving the unique gameplay and the degisn choice of it .The Binding of Isaac has since become a classic, praised for its challenging gameplay, and its unique blend of themes.

Thoughts

The Binding of Isaac is a game that I find incredibly engaging. Each playthrough is unique as the each time played the enemies, item, and layout are different needing to adapt, strategize and prove you can clear each level skillfully. As each win and a playthrough achived you gain new map, items, enemies, characters, and special challeges to beat for the next time you click start which provides a refreshment of new content. This is why I believe The Binding of Isaac deserves its place as the 2nd best game on the list.
